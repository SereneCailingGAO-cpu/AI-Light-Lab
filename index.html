Keep your experiment fair

Use the same phone, surface, distance, and camera angle. Change one thing at a time. Avoid pointing the camera directly at the sun.

What are we measuring?

A relative estimate from average camera-frame brightness. Auto-exposure and white balance can change the result, so readings are not calibrated physical light measurements or directly comparable across phones. No AI model is used.

Camera access

Open this page over HTTPS on your phone, or localhost on the same device. Allow camera access in Safari or Chrome. Camera images are processed locally and are never uploaded. Switching away pauses recording.
